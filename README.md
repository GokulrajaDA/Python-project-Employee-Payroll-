## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses New Zealand's Gross Domestic Product (GDP) from **1972 to June 2026 Quarter**.

The objective is to understand long-term economic growth, industry contribution, consumption patterns, and expenditure trends using the **Production (P), Expenditure (E), and Income (I)** approaches.

**Dataset:** `gross-domestic-product-june-2026-quarter.csv`

---

## 🎯 Objective

- To measure the total value of goods and services produced in the economy.
- To track GDP growth by industry and by type of expenditure over time.
- To analyse trends in Final Consumption, Investment, Exports, Imports, and National Income.
- To support forecasting, policy analysis, academic research, and business planning.
